How it works

From idea to heirloom

A clear, unhurried process so you always know what comes next.

  1. Consultation

    We talk through your vision, budget and timeline — in the studio or by video.

  2. Design

    Sketches and a 3D render or wax model so you can see the piece before it exists.

  3. Craft

    Casting, fabrication, stone setting and finishing, all done by hand at the bench.

  4. Delivery

    Final fitting, care guidance and lifetime cleaning and inspection.

About

Our story

Founded in 2012 and located in Burlington, Jason's The Art of Jewelry has been designing and building jewelry for the people of Alamance County, North Carolina — years and counting. Every commission is treated every commission as a one-of-a-kind work of art. Whether it is a simple repair or a complete custom creation, the same attention goes into every piece that leaves the studio.

Serving Burlington, Graham, Mebane, Elon, Haw River, Gibsonville and neighbors across the county. Working directly with the person who makes your jewelry means honest advice, fair pricing and a piece that is built to last generations.

Questions

FAQ

How long does a custom piece take?

Timing depends on the design and materials. Call or text the shop for an estimate, and ask about rush timelines for weddings and events.

Can you reuse my existing gold and stones?

Yes. We can melt down old gold and reset your existing stones into a new design, which often reduces the cost of a custom piece.

Do you offer repairs while I wait?

Repairs are done in store. Stop by during business hours and we will tell you what the job involves and how long it will take.

Do you ship or work remotely?

Shipping is available. Call or text the shop to arrange an order.

Do you offer financing or layaway?

Yes. Financing options and layaway plans are available — ask in store.
